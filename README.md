# 🌿 FloroNova — AI-Powered Smart Plant Care Assistant

FloroNova is an intelligent, AI-powered plant care assistant that helps users identify, diagnose, and treat plant health issues in real time using AR, computer vision, and multimodal AI.

It acts like a **personal plant doctor** that can detect diseases, pests, nutrient deficiencies, and watering issues instantly and provide structured treatment recommendations.

---

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Many plant owners struggle with:
- Identifying plant species
- Understanding leaf problems (yellowing, spots, dryness)
- Distinguishing between disease, pests, or watering issues
- Getting fast and reliable plant care advice

Traditional solutions are slow, confusing, or require expert knowledge.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

FloroNova solves this using **AI + AR + Multimodal intelligence**:

- Point your camera at a plant → get instant diagnosis
- Upload a plant image → get detailed health analysis
- Chat with AI → ask plant care questions anytime
- Get structured treatment + prevention plans
